Baby Names

Popularity: Molly vs. Michael (Top Male Name in 1991)

In 1991, the most popular male name was Michael with 61,077 babies, coinciding with Molly’s peak year of 4,663 babies. While Molly rose sharply through the 1980s before tapering off, Michael was undergoing a long, steady decline from over 67,000 in 1976 to around 8,100 in 2025.
