Popularity: Molly vs. Olivia (Today’s Top Female Name)

Today’s most popular female name is Olivia (13,554 babies in 2025). While both names started at comparable levels in 1976 (around 1,200 to 1,700 babies), Molly peaked early in 1991, whereas Olivia surged dramatically starting in the late 1990s and has stayed near the top of the charts ever since.

State-by-State Popularity by Year

The map displays the relative balance between Molly (blue) and Olivia (green) across all 50 states from 1976 to 2025, illustrating Molly’s widespread nationwide popularity in the 1980s and 1990s before Olivia gradually overtook nearly every state in the 2000s.
